# The Best Dermatologists in Gurugram for Hyperpigmentation Treatment

Hyperpigmentation, a common skin condition, manifests as darkened patches on the skin. It occurs due to an overproduction of melanin, the pigment responsible for skin color. Understanding the causes, symptoms, and available treatments is crucial for managing this condition effectively. This article highlights leading dermatologists in Gurugram specializing in hyperpigmentation treatment.

## Understanding Hyperpigmentation

*   **Symptoms:** Dark spots or patches on the skin, which can vary in size and color. Common types include melasma, sunspots (solar lentigines), and post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH).
*   **Causes:**
    *   **Sun Exposure:** UV radiation stimulates melanin production.
    *   **Hormonal Changes:** Pregnancy, birth control pills can trigger melasma.
    *   **Inflammation:** Acne, eczema, injuries can lead to PIH.
    *   **Medications:** Certain drugs can increase skin pigmentation.
*   **Treatment Options:** Topical creams (hydroquinone, retinoids, kojic acid), chemical peels, laser therapy, and microdermabrasion.
