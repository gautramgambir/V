# PRP for Baldness: Finding the Top Dermatologist in Gurgaon

Androgenetic alopecia, commonly known as baldness, is a prevalent skin disease affecting both men and women. It's characterized by a gradual thinning of hair, often starting with a receding hairline in men and a widening part in women. Let's delve into the causes, symptoms, and explore how Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy can help, along with some of the best dermatologists in Gurgaon who can assist.

## Understanding Baldness (Androgenetic Alopecia)

*   **Symptoms:** Gradual thinning of hair on the scalp, receding hairline (men), widening hair part (women), increased hair shedding.
*   **Causes:** Primarily genetic predisposition and hormonal factors (androgens). Age can also be a contributing factor.
*   **Diagnosis:** A dermatologist can diagnose baldness through a physical examination of the scalp, medical history, and sometimes blood tests to rule out other underlying conditions.

## PRP Therapy for Hair Loss: A Potential Solution

PRP therapy involves injecting concentrated platelets from your own blood into the scalp. Platelets contain growth factors that can stimulate hair follicles, promoting hair growth and increasing hair thickness.

*   **How it Works:** PRP injections deliver concentrated growth factors directly to the hair follicles, stimulating their activity.
*   **Benefits:** Potentially improved hair growth, increased hair thickness, and reduced hair shedding.
*   **Ideal Candidates:** Individuals in the early stages of hair loss may experience better results.
