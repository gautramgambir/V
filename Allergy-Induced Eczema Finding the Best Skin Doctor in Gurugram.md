# Allergy-Induced Eczema: Finding the Best Skin Doctor in Gurugram

Allergy-induced eczema, also known as allergic contact dermatitis, is a common skin condition causing red, itchy, and inflamed skin. It occurs when your skin comes into contact with a substance that triggers an allergic reaction. Identifying and managing these allergies is crucial for effective treatment.

## Understanding Allergy-Induced Eczema

*   **Symptoms:** Common symptoms include intense itching, dry, scaly patches, small raised bumps, blisters that may ooze and crust, and thickened, leathery skin from prolonged scratching.
*   **Causes:** Common allergens include nickel (in jewelry), fragrances (in perfumes and lotions), preservatives (in cosmetics), latex, certain medications applied topically, and poison ivy or oak.
*   **Diagnosis:** Dermatologists typically diagnose allergy-induced eczema through a physical exam and patch testing to identify the specific allergen.
*   **Treatment:** Treatment usually involves avoiding the identified allergen, using topical corticosteroids or calcineurin inhibitors to reduce inflammation and itch, and keeping the skin well-moisturized.
