## Understanding Botox and Fillers

*   **Botox (Botulinum Toxin):** Primarily used to reduce the appearance of wrinkles caused by muscle movement. It works by temporarily paralyzing the muscles, smoothing out lines and wrinkles. Common areas of treatment include forehead lines, crow's feet, and frown lines.

    *   **Symptoms Addressed:** Forehead wrinkles, crow's feet, frown lines, neck bands.
    *   **Causes of Wrinkles:** Muscle contractions, aging, sun exposure, genetics.
    *   **Procedure:** Involves injecting small amounts of Botox into targeted muscles.
    *   **Duration:** Effects typically last 3-6 months.

*   **Dermal Fillers:** Used to restore volume loss, smooth lines, and enhance facial contours. They are injected into the skin to fill wrinkles, plump lips, and augment cheeks. Fillers are made from various substances, including hyaluronic acid, calcium hydroxylapatite, and poly-L-lactic acid.

    *   **Symptoms Addressed:** Wrinkles, volume loss, thin lips, nasolabial folds, marionette lines.
    *   **Causes of Volume Loss:** Aging, genetics, weight loss.
    *   **Procedure:** Involves injecting filler into the target area.
    *   **Duration:** Varies depending on the type of filler used, but typically lasts 6-18 months.
