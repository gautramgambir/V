## Understanding Chronic Skin Sensitivity

Chronic skin sensitivity, also known as sensitive skin, isn't a specific disease but rather a tendency to experience adverse reactions to substances that typically don't bother others.

**Common Symptoms:**

*   Frequent itching
*   Redness and inflammation
*   Dryness and flakiness
*   Burning or stinging sensations
*   Rashes or bumps
*   Reactions to specific products (cosmetics, soaps, detergents)
*   Increased sensitivity to sun exposure

**Possible Causes:**

*   **Genetic Predisposition:** Some individuals are simply born with more sensitive skin.
*   **Underlying Skin Conditions:** Eczema (atopic dermatitis), rosacea, and psoriasis can all contribute to skin sensitivity.
*   **Environmental Factors:** Harsh weather, pollution, and exposure to irritants can exacerbate sensitivity.
*   **Allergies:** Allergic reactions to certain substances can trigger skin sensitivity.
*   **Irritant Contact Dermatitis:** Repeated exposure to irritants can damage the skin's protective barrier.
*   **Over-Exfoliation:** Excessive use of exfoliating products can strip the skin of its natural oils, leading to sensitivity.
*   **Harsh Skincare Products:** Products containing fragrances, dyes, and harsh chemicals can irritate sensitive skin.

## Treatment Options

A dermatologist can recommend various treatments depending on the cause and severity of your skin sensitivity, including:

*   **Topical Corticosteroids:** To reduce inflammation and itching.
*   **Emollients:** To moisturize and protect the skin barrier.
*   **Antihistamines:** To relieve itching caused by allergies.
*   **Lifestyle Modifications:** Avoiding known irritants, using gentle skincare products, and protecting your skin from the sun.
*   **Prescription Creams and Ointments:** For specific skin conditions like eczema or rosacea.
