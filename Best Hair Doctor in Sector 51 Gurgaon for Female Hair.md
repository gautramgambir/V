# Best Hair Doctor in Sector 51 Gurgaon for Female Hair Loss

Female hair loss, also known as female pattern baldness or androgenetic alopecia, is a common condition characterized by a gradual thinning of hair on the scalp. Unlike male pattern baldness, which often starts with a receding hairline, women typically experience a widening of the part and overall thinning.

## Understanding Female Hair Loss

### Symptoms:

*   Gradual thinning on top of the head
*   Widening part line
*   Increased hair shedding
*   Smaller ponytail circumference

### Causes:

*   **Genetics:** A family history of hair loss increases your risk.
*   **Hormonal Changes:** Menopause, pregnancy, and thyroid disorders can contribute.
*   **Medical Conditions:** Alopecia areata, scalp infections, and lupus can cause hair loss.
*   **Medications and Supplements:** Certain drugs, such as those used for cancer, arthritis, depression, heart problems, and high blood pressure, can have hair loss as a side effect.
*   **Stress:** Significant stress can lead to temporary hair loss.
*   **Hairstyles and Treatments:** Overuse of tight hairstyles or harsh chemical treatments can damage hair follicles.

## Treatment Options

A dermatologist can recommend various treatment options, including:

*   **Topical Medications:** Minoxidil is a common over-the-counter solution.
*   **Prescription Medications:** Finasteride and spironolactone can be prescribed in some cases.
*   **Laser Therapy:** Can stimulate hair growth.
*   **PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) Therapy:** Involves injecting platelets into the scalp to promote hair growth.
*   **Hair Transplant:** A surgical option for more advanced hair loss.
