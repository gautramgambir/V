## Understanding Skin Infections

Skin infections occur when bacteria, fungi, viruses, or parasites invade the skin. These infections can range from mild to severe, impacting different layers of the skin.

### Common Types of Skin Infections:

*   **Bacterial Infections:** Examples include impetigo, cellulitis, and folliculitis. They often appear as red, swollen, and painful areas, sometimes with pus-filled blisters.
*   **Fungal Infections:** Ringworm, athlete's foot, and yeast infections are common. Symptoms can include itching, scaling, and redness.
*   **Viral Infections:** Warts, herpes simplex (cold sores), and shingles are viral skin infections. They may cause blisters, pain, and itching.
*   **Parasitic Infections:** Scabies and lice are parasitic infections that cause intense itching and skin irritation.

### Symptoms of Skin Infections:

*   Redness
*   Itching
*   Swelling
*   Pain
*   Blisters
*   Pus
*   Scaling
*   Rash

### Causes of Skin Infections:

*   Poor hygiene
*   Skin injuries (cuts, scrapes)
*   Weakened immune system
*   Contact with infected individuals or surfaces
*   Underlying medical conditions

## When to See a Dermatologist

If you experience any persistent or severe symptoms of a skin infection, it's crucial to consult a dermatologist. Early diagnosis and treatment can prevent complications and promote faster healing.

## Prevention Tips

*   Maintain good hygiene.
*   Avoid sharing personal items.
*   Keep skin clean and dry.
*   Treat minor cuts and scrapes promptly.
*   Avoid contact with infected individuals.

By understanding skin infections and seeking timely medical attention, you can effectively manage these conditions and maintain healthy, radiant skin.
