# Top Dermatologists in Gurgaon for Skin Discoloration Treatment

Skin discoloration, a common dermatological concern, manifests as uneven skin tone due to variations in melanin production. It can be localized or widespread, impacting appearance and self-esteem.

## Understanding Skin Discoloration

**Symptoms:**

*   Hyperpigmentation: Darker patches (e.g., age spots, melasma).
*   Hypopigmentation: Lighter patches (e.g., vitiligo).
*   Redness: Erythema, often associated with inflammation.
*   Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH): Darkening of the skin after inflammation (e.g., acne).

**Causes:**

*   Sun exposure: A primary trigger for hyperpigmentation.
*   Hormonal changes: Melasma is common during pregnancy.
*   Inflammation: Acne, eczema, or injury can lead to PIH.
*   Genetics: Predisposition to conditions like vitiligo.
*   Medications: Certain drugs can cause pigmentation changes.
*   Skin Infections: Fungal infections like tinea versicolor.

If you're experiencing any of these symptoms, consulting a dermatologist is crucial for accurate diagnosis and effective treatment.
