## What is Molluscum Contagiosum?

Molluscum contagiosum is a viral skin infection that causes small, raised, pearly or flesh-colored bumps on the skin.

*   **Causes:** It's caused by the molluscum contagiosum virus (MCV), a type of poxvirus.
*   **Symptoms:** Small, raised bumps with a central dimple. They can appear anywhere on the body, but are common on the face, neck, armpits, arms, and legs.
*   **Transmission:** Spread through direct skin-to-skin contact, touching contaminated objects, or sexual contact.

## What are Warts?

Warts are skin growths caused by the human papillomavirus (HPV).

*   **Causes:** Different strains of HPV cause different types of warts.
*   **Symptoms:** Rough, raised bumps on the skin. Common warts often appear on the hands and fingers, while plantar warts develop on the soles of the feet.
*   **Transmission:** Spread through direct contact with a wart or a contaminated surface.

## When to See a Dermatologist

While both molluscum contagiosum and warts can sometimes resolve on their own, consulting a dermatologist is recommended in the following situations:

*   The lesions are spreading rapidly.
*   The lesions are causing discomfort or pain.
*   You have a weakened immune system.
*   Home remedies are not effective.
*   You are concerned about the appearance of the lesions.

## Treatment Options

Dermatologists offer various treatment options for molluscum contagiosum and warts, including:

*   **Cryotherapy:** Freezing the lesions with liquid nitrogen.
*   **Curettage:** Scraping off the lesions.
*   **Topical Medications:** Applying creams or solutions to the lesions.
*   **Laser Therapy:** Using lasers to destroy the lesions.
*   **Electrocautery:** Burning off the lesions.
