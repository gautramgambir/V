# Struggling with Cystic Acne? Find the Best Dermatologist Near You in Gurgaon

Cystic acne is a severe form of acne that develops when oil, dead skin cells, and bacteria become trapped deep inside the pores. Unlike typical pimples, cystic acne forms painful, pus-filled bumps under the skin. This inflammatory condition can significantly impact self-esteem and requires specialized dermatological care.

## Understanding Cystic Acne

### Symptoms of Cystic Acne:

*   Large, red, painful bumps under the skin.
*   Pus-filled cysts or nodules.
*   Tenderness and sensitivity around the affected area.
*   Potential scarring.

### Causes of Cystic Acne:

*   **Hormonal Imbalances:** Fluctuations in hormones, especially during puberty, menstruation, or pregnancy, can trigger cystic acne.
*   **Excess Oil Production:** Overactive sebaceous glands produce excess sebum, which can clog pores.
*   **Bacteria:** The bacterium *Cutibacterium acnes* (formerly *Propionibacterium acnes*) can thrive in clogged pores, leading to inflammation and infection.
*   **Genetics:** A family history of acne can increase your susceptibility.
*   **Certain Medications:** Some medications can contribute to acne breakouts.

## Treatment Options for Cystic Acne

A dermatologist can recommend various treatment options depending on the severity of your acne. These may include:

*   **Topical Medications:** Prescription-strength creams and gels containing retinoids, benzoyl peroxide, or antibiotics.
*   **Oral Medications:** Antibiotics, hormonal birth control pills (for women), or isotretinoin (Accutane) for severe cases.
*   **Corticosteroid Injections:** Injecting corticosteroids directly into cysts to reduce inflammation and promote healing.
*   **Laser and Light Therapy:** Using lasers or light to reduce bacteria and inflammation.
*   **Chemical Peels:** Exfoliating the skin to unclog pores and reduce inflammation.
*   **Drainage and Extraction:** Carefully draining large cysts to relieve pain and pressure (should only be performed by a dermatologist).
