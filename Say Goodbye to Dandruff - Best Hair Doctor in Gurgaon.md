# Say Goodbye to Dandruff – Best Hair Doctor in Gurgaon

Dandruff, characterized by flaky skin on the scalp, is a common skin condition affecting people of all ages. While often harmless, it can be itchy and embarrassing. Understanding dandruff, its causes, and available treatments is key to managing and eliminating it.

## Understanding Dandruff: Causes, Symptoms, and Treatment

*   **What is Dandruff?** Dandruff is a form of seborrheic dermatitis that specifically affects the scalp. It results in the shedding of dead skin cells, leading to visible flakes.
*   **Symptoms:**
    *   Flakes of skin on the scalp, hair, eyebrows, beard, or mustache, and shoulders.
    *   Itchy scalp.
    *   Scaly, crusty scalp in infants (cradle cap).
*   **Causes:** The exact cause of dandruff isn't fully understood, but several factors contribute:
    *   **Malassezia:** A yeast-like fungus that lives on the scalp of most adults. Overgrowth can irritate the scalp and cause excess skin cell growth.
    *   **Seborrheic Dermatitis:** An inflammatory skin condition.
    *   **Dry Skin:**  Dryness can lead to flaking and itching.
    *   **Shampooing Habits:** Infrequent or overly frequent shampooing.
    *   **Other Skin Conditions:** Eczema and psoriasis.
    *   **Diet:**  Poor diet lacking certain nutrients.
*   **Treatment:**
    *   **Over-the-counter (OTC) shampoos:**  Look for shampoos containing zinc pyrithione, selenium sulfide, ketoconazole, or salicylic acid.
    *   **Prescription shampoos and treatments:**  If OTC options don't work, a dermatologist can prescribe stronger medications.
    *   **Lifestyle Changes:** Managing stress, eating a balanced diet, and proper hair care.
