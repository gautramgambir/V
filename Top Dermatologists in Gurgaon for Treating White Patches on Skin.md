# Top Dermatologists in Gurgaon for Treating White Patches on Skin (Vitiligo)

White patches on the skin, also known as Vitiligo or Leucoderma, is a skin condition characterized by the loss of melanin, the pigment that gives skin its color. This loss results in depigmented patches that can appear anywhere on the body.

## Understanding Vitiligo: Symptoms, Causes, and Treatment

**Symptoms:**

*   Appearance of milky-white patches on the skin.
*   Patches are often symmetrical.
*   Premature graying or whitening of hair on the scalp, eyelashes, eyebrows, and beard.
*   Loss of color in the tissues that line the inside of the mouth and nose (mucous membranes).

**Causes:**

The exact cause of vitiligo is unknown, but it is believed to be an autoimmune disorder. Other potential contributing factors include:

*   Genetic predisposition
*   Stress
*   Exposure to certain chemicals
*   Sunburn

**Treatment:**

While there's no cure for vitiligo, several treatments can help restore skin color or even out skin tone. Treatment options include:

*   Topical corticosteroids and calcineurin inhibitors
*   Light therapy (phototherapy)
*   Depigmentation (removing color from unaffected skin)
*   Surgery (skin grafting or melanocyte transplants)
