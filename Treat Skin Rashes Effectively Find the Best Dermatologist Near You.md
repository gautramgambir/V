## Understanding Skin Rashes

Skin rashes are characterized by changes in skin color, texture, or appearance. They can be localized to a small area or spread across the body.

**Common Symptoms of Skin Rashes:**

*   Redness
*   Itching
*   Bumps or blisters
*   Dryness or scaling
*   Swelling

**Common Causes of Skin Rashes:**

*   **Allergies:** Reactions to allergens like pollen, pet dander, food, or medications.
*   **Irritants:** Contact with substances that irritate the skin, such as harsh soaps, detergents, or chemicals.
*   **Infections:** Bacterial, viral, or fungal infections (e.g., ringworm, chickenpox).
*   **Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis):** A chronic inflammatory skin condition.
*   **Psoriasis:** An autoimmune condition causing scaly, red patches.
*   **Heat Rash (Miliaria):** Caused by blocked sweat ducts.
*   **Insect Bites:** Reactions to insect bites or stings.

## When to See a Dermatologist

While mild rashes may resolve on their own, it's important to consult a dermatologist if:

*   The rash is severe or widespread.
*   The rash is accompanied by fever or pain.
*   The rash doesn't improve with home remedies.
*   You suspect an allergic reaction.
*   The rash is recurring.
