## Understanding Skin Infections

Skin infections occur when bacteria, fungi, viruses, or parasites invade the skin. These infections can range from mild to severe and may affect any part of the body.

**Common Symptoms:**

*   Redness and inflammation
*   Itching and burning sensations
*   Rashes, blisters, or pus-filled bumps
*   Dry, scaly, or cracked skin
*   Pain and tenderness

**Potential Causes:**

*   Poor hygiene
*   Weakened immune system
*   Exposure to contaminated surfaces
*   Contact with infected individuals
*   Underlying medical conditions

**Types of Skin Infections:**

*   **Bacterial Infections:** Impetigo, cellulitis, folliculitis.
*   **Fungal Infections:** Athlete's foot, ringworm, yeast infections.
*   **Viral Infections:** Warts, herpes, shingles.

## When to See a Dermatologist

If you suspect you have a skin infection, especially if it's severe, persistent, or spreading, consult a dermatologist promptly. Early diagnosis and treatment can prevent complications and promote faster healing. A dermatologist can accurately identify the type of infection and recommend the most appropriate treatment plan for your specific needs. They can provide topical or oral medications, wound care advice, and other interventions to help you recover.
