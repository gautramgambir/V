Pimples, medically known as acne vulgaris, are a common skin condition affecting millions worldwide. Characterized by inflamed and often painful bumps on the skin, acne can impact self-esteem and overall well-being.

## Understanding Pimples: Causes, Symptoms, and Treatment

**What are Pimples (Acne)?**

Acne occurs when hair follicles become clogged with oil and dead skin cells. This can lead to the formation of whiteheads, blackheads, pimples, cysts, and nodules.

**Common Symptoms:**

*   Blackheads: Open pores filled with oil and dead skin.
*   Whiteheads: Closed pores filled with oil and dead skin.
*   Papules: Small, red, raised bumps.
*   Pustules: Papules with pus at their tips (pimples).
*   Nodules: Large, solid, painful lumps beneath the skin.
*   Cystic Lesions: Painful, pus-filled lumps beneath the skin.

**What Causes Acne?**

*   **Excess Oil Production:** Overactive sebaceous glands produce too much sebum.
*   **Clogged Hair Follicles:** Dead skin cells accumulate and block pores.
*   **Bacteria:** *Cutibacterium acnes* bacteria thrive in clogged pores.
*   **Inflammation:** The body's immune response causes redness and swelling.
*   **Hormonal Changes:** Puberty, menstruation, and pregnancy can trigger acne.
*   **Genetics:** A family history of acne increases your risk.
*   **Diet:** Certain foods may trigger breakouts in some individuals.
*   **Stress:** Stress can exacerbate acne.

**When to See a Dermatologist:**

If over-the-counter treatments don't improve your acne, or if you have severe acne, consult a dermatologist. Dermatologists can provide prescription medications, advanced treatments, and personalized skincare recommendations.
