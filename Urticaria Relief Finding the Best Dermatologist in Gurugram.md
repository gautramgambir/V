# Urticaria Relief: Finding the Best Dermatologist in Gurugram

Urticaria, commonly known as hives, is a skin condition characterized by itchy, raised welts that appear on the skin. These welts can vary in size and shape and often appear in clusters. Understanding urticaria is crucial for effective management and relief.

**Symptoms of Urticaria:**

*   Itchy welts (wheals) on the skin
*   Welts that blanch (turn white) when pressed
*   Welts that can change shape, move around, and disappear within 24 hours
*   Swelling (angioedema), especially around the eyes, lips, or throat (less common but potentially serious)

**Causes of Urticaria:**

Urticaria can be triggered by various factors, including:

*   **Allergens:** Foods (nuts, shellfish, eggs), medications (antibiotics, NSAIDs), insect stings
*   **Infections:** Viral, bacterial, or fungal infections
*   **Physical Stimuli:** Pressure, cold, heat, sunlight, exercise
*   **Underlying Medical Conditions:** Autoimmune diseases, thyroid disorders
*   **Stress:** Emotional stress can sometimes trigger or worsen urticaria.

**When to Seek a Dermatologist:**

While mild cases of urticaria may resolve on their own or with over-the-counter antihistamines, it's essential to consult a dermatologist if:

*   Symptoms are severe or persistent (lasting more than a few days)
*   Urticaria is accompanied by angioedema (swelling)
*   Over-the-counter medications don't provide relief
*   You suspect a specific trigger but are unsure
*   The cause of urticaria is unknown
