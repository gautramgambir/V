# Top Skin Allergy Specialists in Gurugram: Find Relief from Itchy Skin

Skin allergies are a common problem, causing discomfort and affecting quality of life. These allergies occur when your immune system overreacts to a harmless substance (allergen). This can manifest as a rash, itching, hives, or other skin irritations.

**Symptoms of Skin Allergies:**

*   Itching
*   Redness
*   Rashes
*   Hives (raised, itchy welts)
*   Dry, scaly skin
*   Blisters

**Common Causes of Skin Allergies:**

*   **Contact Dermatitis:**  Caused by direct contact with irritants like soaps, detergents, jewelry, cosmetics, or poison ivy.
*   **Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis):** A chronic condition often linked to genetics and immune system dysfunction.
*   **Hives (Urticaria):**  Can be triggered by food allergies, medications, insect bites, or infections.
*   **Allergic Contact Dermatitis:** An allergic reaction to specific substances like nickel or fragrances.

**Treatment Options:**

A dermatologist can recommend various treatment options based on the type and severity of your skin allergy, including:

*   Topical creams and ointments (corticosteroids, antihistamines)
*   Oral antihistamines
*   Immunotherapy (allergy shots)
*   Lifestyle modifications to avoid triggers
