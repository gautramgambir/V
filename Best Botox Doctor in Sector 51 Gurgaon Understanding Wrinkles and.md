# Best Botox Doctor in Sector 51 Gurgaon: Understanding Wrinkles and Finding the Right Expert

Botox, or Botulinum Toxin injections, is a popular cosmetic procedure used to reduce the appearance of wrinkles. These wrinkles, often caused by repeated muscle contractions from facial expressions (frown lines, crow's feet), appear due to aging, sun exposure, and genetics.

**Understanding Wrinkles:**

*   **Causes:** Aging (loss of collagen and elastin), sun damage, genetics, repeated facial expressions.
*   **Symptoms:** Fine lines and wrinkles on the face, particularly around the eyes, forehead, and mouth.
*   **Botox Treatment:** Botox works by temporarily paralyzing the muscles that cause wrinkles, resulting in smoother skin.
