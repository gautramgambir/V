## Understanding Skin Allergies

Skin allergies, also known as allergic dermatitis, occur when your skin reacts adversely to a substance. This reaction can manifest in various ways, causing discomfort and affecting quality of life.

### Symptoms of Skin Allergies:

*   Redness
*   Itching
*   Rash
*   Hives
*   Dry, cracked skin
*   Blisters
*   Swelling

### Common Causes of Skin Allergies:

*   **Allergens:** Pollen, pet dander, certain foods.
*   **Irritants:** Soaps, detergents, chemicals, perfumes.
*   **Contact Dermatitis:** Direct contact with an allergen or irritant.
*   **Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis):** A chronic condition often linked to allergies.
*   **Urticaria (Hives):** Often triggered by food or medication allergies.
