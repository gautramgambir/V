# Eczema Treatment in Gurgaon: Finding the Right Dermatologist

Eczema, also known as atopic dermatitis, is a chronic skin condition characterized by dry, itchy, and inflamed skin. It's a common condition, particularly in children, but it can affect people of all ages. Finding the right dermatologist is crucial for effective eczema management.

**Understanding Eczema:**

*   **Symptoms:** Intense itching (especially at night), dry, cracked, scaly skin, small raised bumps, thickened skin (lichenification), raw, sensitive skin from scratching.
*   **Causes:** While the exact cause isn't fully understood, eczema is likely a combination of genetic predisposition, immune system dysfunction, and environmental triggers.
*   **Triggers:** Common triggers include allergens (pollen, pet dander), irritants (soaps, detergents), stress, weather changes, and certain foods.

**When to See a Dermatologist:**

If over-the-counter treatments aren't providing relief, or if your eczema is severe, widespread, or affecting your quality of life, consult a dermatologist. A dermatologist can diagnose your specific type of eczema, identify potential triggers, and develop a personalized treatment plan.

**Eczema Treatment Options:**

A dermatologist may recommend one or more of the following treatments:

*   **Topical Corticosteroids:** To reduce inflammation and itching.
*   **Moisturizers:** Essential for keeping the skin hydrated and preventing dryness.
*   **Topical Calcineurin Inhibitors:** Non-steroidal creams that reduce inflammation.
*   **Antihistamines:** To relieve itching, especially at night.
*   **Phototherapy (Light Therapy):** Using ultraviolet light to reduce inflammation.
*   **Biologic Injections:** For severe eczema that doesn't respond to other treatments.
*   **Lifestyle Modifications:** Identifying and avoiding triggers, using gentle skincare products, and managing stress.
